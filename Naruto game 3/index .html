How to play

  1. Choose Practice Mode to learn each jutsu without any pressure.
  2. Perform the hand signs in front of your camera, in the exact order shown for the jutsu.
  3. When the full sequence is recognised, the jutsu animation plays.
  4. A wrong sign resets the sequence. Start again from the first sign.
  5. In Battle Mode, connect with a rival on the same Wi-Fi. Aim your hand crosshair at their head and unleash your jutsu!
